# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Technical hiring managers evaluating a take-home assignment. They'll spend 5-10 minutes clicking around and judging technical creativity and product instincts.

## Product Purpose

AI voice presenter that narrates slides, answers questions, and navigates conversationally. A working prototype demonstrating voice-based AI interaction patterns.

## Brand Personality

Bold, minimal, technical.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards
- Enterprise/corporate density
- Dark mode AI startup aesthetic

## Design Principles

1. The slide is the hero. Everything else supports it.
2. Every element earns its place.
3. Technical confidence through typography and spacing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA text contrast. Keyboard accessible.
